03 How It Works

Simple, seamless workflow

Four steps from first message to completed appointment.

01

Patient sends a WhatsApp message

A patient texts your clinic WhatsApp number — the AI receives the message instantly and understands intent.

02

AI replies instantly

The assistant responds naturally — answering FAQs, providing clinic hours, and addressing patient questions in real time.

03

AI books the appointment or answers questions

For booking requests, the AI checks availability, reserves the slot, and confirms the appointment back to the patient.

04

Staff take over if needed

Complex or sensitive inquiries are flagged for your team. The AI passes over full context so staff can respond without repeating the patient.
